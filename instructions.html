How to Claim Your Tesla

Step-by-step instructions

Step 1

Browse Vehicle Gallery

Start by exploring our complete Tesla vehicle lineup with 20+ models.

Step 2

Select Your Dream Vehicle

Choose the Tesla model you want. Click "Claim Now" on any vehicle card.

Step 3

Fill Out Claim Form

Complete your personal and delivery information. All * fields are required.

Step 4

Review Your Order

Verify all details and choose your delivery method at checkout.

Step 5

Delivery Options

  • 📦 Standard: $299 (10-14 days)
  • 🚚 Express: $349 (5-7 days)
  • 🚀 Premium: $399 (3-5 days)
Step 6

Complete Payment

Choose payment method (Credit Card or Apple Gift Card) and complete payment securely.

Step 7

Confirmation & Tracking

Receive confirmation number. Track your delivery on the Live Deliveries page.

💡 Important Tips

  • Double-check all information before submitting
  • Provide accurate delivery address
  • Keep email and phone updated
  • One entry per household only
  • Save your confirmation number
  • Check spam folder for confirmation email

🔧 Troubleshooting

Form won't submit?

Check all * fields are filled. Verify email/phone format is valid.

Payment declined?

Check card details. Verify sufficient funds. Try different card or contact your bank.

No confirmation email?

Check spam folder. Wait 5 minutes. Contact support if still missing.
